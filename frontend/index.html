How NitoCAD Works

From plain text to production CAD in seconds.

Submit your engineering specification in natural language and receive an auto-validated CAD model with full parametric traceability.

01

Describe

Enter specifications in plain English: dimensions, hole patterns, fillets, wall thickness, and materials. DeepSeek extracts exact numerical parameters with robust fallback parsers.

02

Generate & Validate

CadQuery and OpenCASCADE synthesize precise B-rep solid geometry. An automated validation engine detects geometric violations (e.g., oversized fillets or thin walls) and auto-corrects them before export.

03

Export & Manufacture

Receive true CAD solids (.STEP, .IGES) for immediate manufacturing or import into SolidWorks, Fusion 360, or Inventor, alongside lightweight 3D previews (.STL).
